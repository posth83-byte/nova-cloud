# Nordic Varda – supplier due-diligence request (draft, not sent)

Recipient: office@mondex.pl / b2b@mondexhome.com
Subject: Dropshipping cooperation – Nordic Varda (Sweden)

Hello,

We are evaluating MONDEX as a potential supplier for Nordic Varda, a Sweden-focused online home and interior store using a custom-built storefront.

Before applying or signing an agreement, could you please confirm the following in writing?

1. **Sweden delivery**
   - Can you ship dropship orders to private customers in Sweden?
   - Please provide parcel rates/weight bands, carrier options, typical transit time and any surcharges.
   - Since orders use the retailer's shipping label, can you support a Swedish carrier label (PDF/ZPL, 100 × 150 mm) and which carriers/brokers work reliably with your warehouse?

2. **Product catalogue**
   - Can you provide a sample XML/CSV feed and API documentation before activation?
   - Please include 20–30 non-electrical home decor, storage and kitchen accessory items suitable for Sweden.
   - For each item, please include SKU, EAN, wholesale price excluding VAT, stock quantity, dimensions, packed weight, carton dimensions, product images, product description, manufacturer, EU responsible person (where applicable), GPSR warnings/instructions and country of origin.
   - Please identify products with low breakage risk and confirm packaging standards for fragile goods.

3. **Commercial terms**
   - Please confirm the current €50 net refundable activation fee, the €250 net purchase threshold for its credit, and the €0.88 net packing/material fee per order.
   - Are there any other account, feed, API, payment, handling or minimum charges?
   - Please confirm invoice/payment terms for a newly registered Swedish business and whether a valid Swedish VAT number is required before onboarding.

4. **Returns and complaints**
   - Please confirm the exact process and costs for defective, damaged-in-transit and customer change-of-mind returns from Sweden.
   - Can you provide a Swedish return address or a practical EU return workflow?
   - Who pays return freight in each case, and what evidence is required for a damage claim?

5. **Custom storefront integration**
   - Our store is not Shopify or WooCommerce. Can we use your API or XML/CSV feed directly?
   - Is there an API sandbox/test account?
   - Can stock and wholesale prices be refreshed automatically, and can orders be submitted by API?
   - Can you provide webhook/event support or a documented polling interval?
   - Can the order workflow accept a customer-specific shipping label and return tracking data?

6. **Product media and compliance**
   - Please confirm the EU licence for product photos/descriptions and any usage restrictions.
   - Please confirm that GPSR manufacturer/responsible-person details and safety information are supplied for every relevant SKU.

We will not list products until the exact SKU, wholesale cost, shipping cost, stock feed, returns process and compliance information have been reviewed.

Kind regards,
Nordic Varda
Sweden
